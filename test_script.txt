Many small businesses pour money into marketing without ever seeing a return, and the first major mistake is chasing every platform at once. Be consistent on ONE channel where your ideal customer hangs out.

The second mistake is selling features instead of outcomes. Speak to the customer's pain, not your tools.

Third, ignoring follow-up kills trust. Slow response means losing customers; answer fast.

Fourth, pretending you don't have competitors. Own your competition, show where you're stronger.

Finally, hiding prices behind "get a quote" drives buyers away. Publish real pricing or a starting range.
